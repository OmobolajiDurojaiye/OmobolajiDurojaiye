### 🛠️ Current Project Spotlight: CreateShop

I’m currently building **CreateShop**, an all-in-one no-code platform that empowers business owners, freelancers, and service providers to create customizable online shops without needing developers.

-   🛍️ **Feature-Rich Shops:** Manage product listings, service bookings, and client interactions seamlessly.
-   📊 **Powerful Dashboards:** Business owners get a dedicated dashboard to manage their entire shop.
-   🎨 **Customizable Templates:** Customers enjoy a smooth storefront experience with multiple free & paid templates.
-   📱 **PWA & Mobile Ready:** Designed as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for easy installability, with plans for a Flutter WebView mobile app.

*Status: In active development. Stay tuned for the first release!*
